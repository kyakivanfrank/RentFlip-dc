01 — Brand
Identity & principle
RentFlip
Easy tenant–landlord relationships
The mark is two interlocking forms — rent flipping between tenant and landlord — in brand purple and deep indigo. Place it on white or light surfaces for full contrast. Always pair with the Plus Jakarta Sans wordmark; never stretch or recolour the glyph.

Line icon set
